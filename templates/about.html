During the final years of military radio communication, operators relied on REP-7 to transmit sensitive information. Only trained personnel understood the transmission order. Modern encryption was considered unreliable by several senior operators, who continued using legacy techniques long after digital systems became standard.

Following the migration to the new communication network, multiple transmission fragments were misplaced. Many recovered documents remain incomplete.

Recovered Notes

  • Several archives were intentionally relocated.
  • Transmission order matters more than decoding method.
  • Old operators rarely documented passwords directly.
  • One missing fragment was recovered years later.
  • Not every document is trustworthy.
